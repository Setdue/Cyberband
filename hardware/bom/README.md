# Bill of Materials

Store the bill of materials and part research here.

Candidate requirements from planning include an ESP32 development board, a small OLED display (the earlier notes considered an SSD1306 128×64 I²C module), a temperature and humidity sensor, buttons, visual or sound or vibration alert components, and portable power with battery monitoring. These are candidates, not a verified shopping list; exact models, quantities, and compatibility remain to be confirmed.

For each selected item, record its role, exact part or module, quantity, voltage/interface, source, and verification status. Do not mark a part as purchased or tested unless that has been confirmed.
